The card of the week: **VI of Swords**
![VI of Swords](/static/images/Swords06.jpg)
---

Another card of the sword suit! The element in correspondence with swords is air, which is symbolic of thought and decision. It's an interesting follow-up to our draw last week - III of Swords reversed. Considering this I feel we can put a pulse on the theme so far this month: **reflection**. This previous week prompted us to engage intimately with ourselves, especially so regarding our sustained wounds while not being free of the situation in which they festered. This week we have an indication of moving forward. We may very well have departed our stagnating storm. I promote optimism regarding the week ahead if not relief, the progress is welcomed even if our destination remains obscured and unknown. Reflect on that which you have departed. Change, for better or worse, is welcome as at least we now have some movement in these waters of which you have some control. Continue your reflection and take time to ponder where you would like this week to arrive. You're certainly not alone in this, be sure to take care of your passengers on this journey and guide the way, even if you are unsure of where you are headed. Take time to acknowledge who joins you, and take care of your relationship to them, it's in your hands. Let your creativity guide you in this, and trust your intuition. Confidence is in order this week, but not certainty, so I'd be wary of acting on impulse; the skies are gray and your decision cannot be guaranteed to result in which you hoped for. So be cautious and reflect on where you'd like this week to take you. Be ready to take action upon arrival. Expect a change to come and be quick to acknowledge it. By this weeks' end you will arrive at a destination of joy, indifference or dismay. Know this weeks' journey was through necessity.
