Pulling a card a week for the week, the question being what to expect?

Three of Swords Reversed. Should I be an optimist here? The card is positive when reversed, as the heart sits seemingly away from the rain and floats above the clouds. It's as if the rain is more so behind in this view, not above. We may be feeling as thought we're standing out of the rain, but still surrounded by it as such.  The swords have yet to be drawn from the heart, so the punctures are not wounds. This urges caution, which should be taken very seriously. A lack of precision in removing the offending swords could make the situation worse. For this week, it's important to appreciate relief, yet remain mindful as to what caused the harm and recognize a situation as improving. Take time to recognize the areas which feel lighter, yet don't feel removed from a situation or make haste decisions, we're not out of the storm and we could very well make a situation worse. This week will bring much needed relief in areas of strife, turmoil and stagnation.
