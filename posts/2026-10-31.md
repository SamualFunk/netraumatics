== Being | Alienation ==

Tending to ourselves and our betterment may follow inline with the old adage frequently and falsely attributed to Einstein: 'Insanity is doing the same thing, over and over again but expecting different results'. Cause and effect rarely follow in predictable manner in development of our interior. We expect a consistent overcoming: an overcoming of ourselves, predicament, nature or social status and inevitably collapsing into the need to once again conduct the ritual of betterment. This return is the insanity of our psyche, we yet fall victim to ourselves evermore. This constant leads us to the fundamental nature of being beings and being beings amongst other beings in their being. Alienation characterizes this insanity through avoidance in our retreat and return.

All this hints at an inherent nothing emanating from our being. We tend a wasteland to which meaning has to be embedded, either through the inheritance of societal norms passed by our parents and institutions or by the continual working of our internal and ever developing moral law. We sit in relation to others in lack, the negativity of which prompts us to grab our tools and labor ourselves to be like or unlike. Symptomatic of such is our anxiety, which in Kierkegaard's definition is 'a dizziness of freedom'. We have a freedom to redefine and reinterpret our being ad infinitum, and forever place something in the void to avoid alienation. Becoming displays the appearances of insanity, but the return to being is its kernel. It's not to overcome our alienation or fill its void, but this in dizziness of freedom is a more productive negotiation with the other. 

Mine and your being is taking place within an obvious state of societal decline. Many could point to the moment in which decline became noticeable to their eye. A recognition of an out-of-place whose announcing itself exposed a new expectation. I recall a moment when I was accused of being a slave for wearing a mask at a grocery store during the beginning stages of COVID. A friend was, more vulgarly, accused of being a proctologist for wearing a mask at the same store (odd right?). Maybe I didn't get out of the house enough prior to those times and certainly didn't during, but the encounter was abrasive and semblances have been thick as of late. That set the stage for a new era. I'm sure a reader or two will point out we've been in decline for sometime prior to COVID - War on Terror, Reagan, Bretton Woods collapse, Vietnam conflict and so on. It's not about racing to the starting the line of historical indicators but identifying these hints that strike the individual the moment they occur. This may have not even occurred for the reader yet, things may be hunky dory and future bright for them...but I highly doubt it. Jarring, shaking moments as such can be identified as an encounter with the Real, moments that cannot be symbolically placed in the greater order. Moments that reveal the alienation of being a being within the symbolic order.    

These Real encounters, in branding your psyche, offer the means through which alienation can prompt betterment of the individual. These are wholly our own, and are not always of a negative encounter - the Real could have revealed itself in the reflections of the sun while fishing with your grandfather as a child. These moments that forgo reconciliation promote being as such, simply being. The content of recollections may not even be important, or accurate to the actual event, but the spacelessness in which they throw us, even in recollection, cement the uneasiness of our interactions. Experiences of symbolic lapse don't need to be reconciled, instead occasionally letting them live freely and manifest in imaginative poetics is appropriate. In reconciliation, we tend to force a mismatched puzzle piece into place. Symbolic placement is done through the throes of ideology, which prescribe to us our notions of right/wrong and determine what can be defined as hostile. In being defined as a slave for wearing a mask, I could assume the placement of the accuser into a libertarian theme. I could even suggest anarchic, though such a comment lambastes the individual for making such a decision for themselves, but in his mind was directed at my submission to a overreaching state in which I have yet to leave the cave. The anarchist always tightens the chains of their own ideology and even so the one on receiving end of the rage is forced to tighten them even further. I'd leave my chains for his perhaps? The one who is not fooled is usually fooled the most in these cases. Anyways and as such, I aim my judgement towards him. I don't think this comes down to any notion of loving thy neighbor, loving thy enemy or loving anything for that matter. Instead I suppose indifference is in order, as the whole situation was seriously unserious. Though my assumptions listed prior may be factually incorrect, as I assume his were, the indifference lies not in letting things be, but in the recognition that we both occupied a time of rapid encounters of the Real. We were both in a position of radical uncertainty, and I believing what was for the common good and he in his rebellion, met at the junction of our collapsed orders. At this junction is the uneasiness we feel as subjects, is this dizziness of freedom and alienation and here is a certain nothing of a wasteland. 

The seemingly sane move in such a position is a retreat, avoid which presents itself as hostile in this symbolic collapse. I can find repose in my sanctuary of belief and claim to avoid the insanity of trying to navigate this impasse. My inclination to label COVID as an accelerant of our decline is due to my observation of heightened retreat inwards, towards symbolic reconciliation qua ideology. The few commons we have, where we were obliged to interact with others not sharing our beliefs, were shuddered. We worked from home if lucky, shunned public places, relied on delivery and maintained a distance when it was not possible to avoid others. These measures, while a completely valid response to the unknown affects of a virus run rampant, manifested in people symptoms we're still trying to work through. We are ever more in the throes of claiming we're the sane ones and they are the ones absorbed into redundant, reactionary, failed or repetitive idealogical systems. The alienation we feel, on this uneasy ground, is the means to breach back into civility. We're obliged to try, over and over, and perhaps while continually reliving a failure to correspond and relate, but the alienation is the very means. Our continual retreats into comfort of ideology is the insane impasse; our idealogical inclinations are at their core unserious when in direct/immediate access to the other.  

Our socially anxious and alienated being is complimented by correspondence. Don't expect to find common ground and try to stop enjoying the enforcement of your 'universal truth'; the void of alienation filled with ideology is unfruitful. Alienation should be a dizziness of freedom in being amongst beings.
