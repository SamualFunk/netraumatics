###**==The Ethics of Pain==**###
---
![Ethics of Pain](/static/images/ethics_pain.jpg)
"Show me your pain and I will tell you what kind of man you are!" - Ernst Jünger, On Pain
---
A perspective on pain takes insight not into its effective quality, say extracting a lesson from its experience, but instead arise under the circumstance of its relation between subject and society. Approaches with the former risk sentiment and silly maxims such as 'soft men create hard times, hard times create hard men' or something like that. The idealized perspective of tolerance gained through and of quarrel arises from the analysis, and lays pain strictly within an empirical passing of something bound to be repeated and compared. It comes and with it goes a breed of man whose enjoyment rests in identifying as 'hard'.Pain is both a consistency and proximity; a matter of infliction and endurance. The luxury of pain as the production of masculine identity or as the point of separation between genders is akin to economic affordance of comfort; I earn, therefor I have earned. A quantitative assessment of pain, and comfort for that matter, need to be understood through the guise of progress through a liberalized society and its implications to the social milieu.             

Pain & progress can be understood through a few notions:

  - Progress through and of pain 
  - Progress without pain
  - Pain through and of progress
  - Pain without progress

The unison of both pain and progress come at the sake of a what and why. When discussing a circumstance of what, what is it that presents as painful, we inevitably have to justify it with a why and thereon to the what-of the why. The explanation of pain has a motion of being cut short in explanation and sealed in tautology: 'it is what it is'. A look at this must therefor not be through depth, as in uncovering layers until it presents as sealed and answered, but through the appearance of the form to uncover the general whatness. Progress itself is unannounced, its an entity of persistence that remains in guise under that which does the announcing - the symptoms, thresholds of discomfort, itches that cannot be scratched, throbbing, a sudden dropping of your stomach to the depths. Its potentiality is always present, yet unrecognized until symptomatic. This takes a course of action to understanding why pain is a persistent symptom of progress and to define each in their isolated regard.

Progress, or liberalized progress, is suggestive of an end point that arrives upon an absolute image; a picture perfect to which we are moving: telos. This absolute is the phenomena of in which progress is illuminated as symptoms, or appearances. This absolute is engaged in a production of its symptoms, like a dealer after shuffling his cards places them for you to see. The intention of the card game is to win; the intention of the phenomena is calling to your arrival at an image of gained wealth. The game might not be as fruitful as you expected, each draw a bust, but you continue on in hopes of the win, possibly at great cost. You may even win a few hands, which prompts further participation. Progress witnessed rests in similar fashion in which its appearances, good or bad, promote its very transcendence.

As the dealer deals his cards, so too do the productive elements of the absolute image. That what does the production is engaged in a quantitative offering producing the appearances whose quality is a reinforcing or denying in relation to the image. A denying appearance is not the refusal of an appearance to emerge, but an appearance signaling the denial of the absolute's arrival. Take for instance the arrival of commercial supersonic travel, the Concorde. The appearance of the Concorde was an affirmation of the imagined technological transcendental. The high production costs and the fatal Air France crash in 2001 appeared as denials of that image's viability. In liberal society, the tools of the production are those which engage in equalizing society to a primal equal: democracy, public services, and capitalism. Each offers positive and negative appearances of its telos. The 1990s with the collapse of the authoritative Soviet Union offered a massive affirmation of liberal progress. So much so prompting Francis Fukuyama's assessment that we've arrived at it's absolute to which he declared 'the end of history'. The assessment of which, in our tumultuous 2020s, has been denied.  

When viewed as as equalizer of no race, no religion or superficial limitation - liberal progress aims to be inclusive of one that requires participation. It infers that the primal quality of each individual is level. The actual equalizing the of the initiatives are in constant flux and in contradiction through each entity's appearances of affirmation and negation. Democracy is affirmed by electing obscene figures who despise it. Public services can provide for basic needs, but access to them needs to be argued and require a hoard of bureaucrats to manage. Capitalism is for the individual to freely contribute and benefit, but through the potential of their assets. On paper you have a system which keeps a level playing field, but is negated by and large through conflicting ideologies, upkeep and hoarding of assets by classes. All three are in delicate balance with each other with each responding to the others perceived overreach. Liberalism cannot be defined without these components, but is made exceptional in one: capitalism. With each able to accumulate and invest in principle based not on any inherent quality of the individual, we can see some have more assets while most have less. This accumulation, or having, in turn embeds itself into a plus one on the primal quality of the individual negated by democracy and public services. So and so is equal in principle, but on paper they lack the assets that ultimately affects the potentiality of this equality. For example, if a debtor is behind on payments, they're assigned a score which ultimately assumes a qualitative assessment of their character, even in regards to circumstance out of their control. This score will be unequal to the debtor who maintains debt in good standing. We now have two beings who retain a primal quality, the non-factors of their person, but assigned a qualitative plus to differentiate. The debtor in bad standing is unable to accumulate and compete with the others which in turn becomes his identity. The potential of accumulating is directly impacted at the mercy of the already accumulated. Class defined primacy is dictated by those-who-have as opposed to those-who-lack. 

What is the absolute image yet to be arrived at in liberal progress? Is liberalism so much so defined by equalizing or by spurring the ability of those who accumulate to accumulate? Liberalism is often misunderstood in the developed west, we see it as an equalizer akin to keeping a level playing field, a furthering of ensuring no cultural or traditional plus ones can be assigned the primal quality of each individual. The appearance of a gay man being denied a job because of his sexuality is of non-equalizing fashion yet the appearance of a gay man in a powerful position of accumulation is a success measure to be pursued. More gay men of power as a metric of equalizing? We see here a limit to how we can define the equalizing aspects of liberal society, limits which are defined by its pivot point - capitalism. As Marx put it, the limits of capitalism is capital itself. We see this is in its equalizing form, as everyone is free to partake through the process in which its driven by acting in continual accumulation. Liberal progress is the pushing of these very limits by the competition spurred in participants via their will to accumulate, but only through its perceived form of being equalizer. Each and all in a competition of comparison among ourselves. The end point is suggestive because of its boundlessness and thus has to manifest itself in its potentiality, an image which prompts the will to accumulate. We're all equal in this will to inflict unequality. Liberalism's ultimate image in progress is the total and final flattening of superficial indicators (race, creed, gender, sexuality, class etc) qua capitalism and capitalism's many innovations to enforce this objective. Democracy and public services are not the issues here by any means, nor is how any person identifies, all should be welcome as all are equal. Liberalism's primary failure rests is seeing capitalism as a solution to capitalism. 

We've arrived at pain - a symptom under the phenomena of progress - being both the affirmative and denial of this image as such. Capitalism drives its telos by being the spear that caused the wound as the very means to heal it. In this, we see promises of progress through and of pain. For example, AI will deliver the ability to enhance your productivity and value, but will be the excuse as to why you were laid off. The pain of AI is one of many examples felt communally under a liberalized society in its pursuits of equalizing via assigning productive value to individuals while promising to overcome it. A data center contributes the infrastructure to enhance our productive output and economic standing, but at the sake of clean water, clean air and silence. The uncertainty resulting from such initiatives of this are bromide comments surrounding universal basic income and utopian luxury achieved through industrial breakthroughs. Surely anything resulting from this is not of benefit to anyone but to those who make their billions through such speculation. This in turn keeps us poor as they live out their fantasies. Is the pain we experience in class a directive to overcome our status by exceeding our imposed placement through hoarding and innovation? Maybe we can find a near holistic experience of actual pain as something to take the mind off progress being or not being had in these platitudes. The individual and their community can't escape society's accelerating progress towards its failure of an absolute, but the individual can be ethical on how they relate to both. I'm at fault if I place my hand on a hot burner as I'm at fault if my focus on the arts keeps me uncompetitive in society. We're at fault if we refuse the perceived economic benefits from erecting a data center in our community for the sake of our peace and natural resources, as this is a choice driven by the collective of individuals adhering to where they stake their ethics. If you feel it's not your choice or out of your control, then dedicate yourself to fighting it tooth and nail; that's the choice you have. The ethics of pain, an ethical commitment that rests solely within the individual and the individual's will - a radical commitment to something which in itself is the very induction of your pain; a direct ownership of pain without comparison; this is what must be accepted as opposed to pursing societal telos. We're not moving towards any absolute, whether it be communism or technological luxury and especially not through any comfortable means. In pursuing these, we justify society's imposition of progress qua pain. We can't expect to make progress without pain as we can't accept having pain without progress. Liberal progress towards its absolute promises the former while delivering the latter. It's the individuals choice to decide how they'd like to proceed - adopting societal telos to find your comfort or having the individual will to reject it.      

Pain through and of progress is an inward motion, a commitment of an individual not to pain as such, but to being very source of it. A directing your will to a self-defined purpose. Life isn't a directive to be happy or to find the happiness in pursuit of comfort; life is a directive to pursue a positive objective of your making - a creation of your own and a goal to which you strive. If this is in betterment to your community, then pursue it wholly. When you direct yourself to a cause, the appearance of pain is not in comparison to anyone else or a call to rid yourself of it, but to work through it via your will to see progress in your own light. Pain is the very indicator of progress of the self. Commit yourself to one thing, commit yourself to something, commit yourself to anything! You can manage this pain. Commit yourself to the promises of having comfort and the pain will become the very thing which must be compared. That's not to say you will be the originator of all pain. We can't forgo society wholly as to escape from it is unlikely, and life is largely out of our control. But when you dedicate the source of your pain, you own your self and your directive.
